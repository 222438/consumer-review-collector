# Presentation Notes

## Three rules to explain

1. `record_id` is the deduplication key. When an ID appears more than once, keep its first source row and record later occurrences in the cleaning log.
2. Missing product name, invalid/missing price, invalid/missing publication date, and unknown brand prevent comparison, so those rows are removed. Optional invalid metrics remain blank rather than being invented.
3. Currency, date, brand and count formats are normalized only when the value can be interpreted under a documented rule. Scores and counts outside the data dictionary bounds are not treated as valid measurements.

## Limitation

All values were generated by code. A high completeness rate and legal ranges show structural quality only; they do not make the sample representative of a market or real consumer behavior.

## Version

Submission package: `v0.3`. Raw data: 408 rows. Clean data: 385 rows. The generator uses seed `20261027` and will not overwrite an existing raw output.
